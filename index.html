Our Services

Central Roofing - Your One-Stop Roofing Hub!

Central Roofing is your trusted partner for top-quality roofing solutions at unbeatable prices. We specialize in manufacturing and expertly installing a wide range of roofing materials, including durable aluminum roofing sheets, elegant coated tiles, reliable Aluzinc roofing sheets, efficient PVC rain gutters, and stylish shingles.

With our commitment to excellence, you can be confident in the longevity and beauty of your roof. Contact us today for exceptional roofing services that stand the test of time.

Production of Roofing Sheets

Roofing sheets are commonly made from aluminium and Aluzinc metals. We run these materials through specialized machines to form durable wave-like profiles, with each sheet type shaped to its own precise and beautiful finish.

Installation of Roofing Sheets

Our installation team measures and cuts sheets accurately, secures them from the bottom upward, and overlaps each sheet correctly to help prevent leaks. Every installation is completed with care and precision.

Free Estimation

We assess the project scope, including roof size, labour, materials, and other expenses, then provide a clear cost estimate so you know what to expect before committing.

Sales of Roofing Sheets

We help customers choose the right roofing sheets for their projects, considering material, size, application, price, installation guidance, and after-sales support.

Repairs & Re-roofing

We repair leaks, rust, and loose sheets, and can replace ageing or extensively damaged roofs with new materials installed safely and professionally.

Reliable Transportation Services

We deliver roofing sheets, tiles, and accessories safely and on time across Ghana. Orders are handled with care, loaded securely, and transported through flexible delivery or customer pickup options.
